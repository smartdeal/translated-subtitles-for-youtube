# Privacy Policy: Translated Subtitles for YouTube

Last updated: 2026-11-25

## Summary
The extension does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics and no accounts.

## What the extension reads
- On `www.youtube.com` pages, it reads the subtitle (caption) track and basic video details (video id, available caption and audio languages) of the video you are watching. YouTube serves this data to your browser anyway; the extension requests it from YouTube the same way the YouTube player does.
- It does not read your YouTube account, history, comments, or any other website.

## How translation works
Subtitles are translated on your device by Chrome's built-in Translator API. The text is not sent to the extension developer or to any third-party translation service. The first time you use a language pair, Chrome itself downloads a translation model from Google; this is a Chrome feature and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

## What is stored
- Settings (languages, display mode, font size) in `chrome.storage.sync`. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome syncs them between your browsers through your Google account.
- A cache of translated subtitle lines per video in `chrome.storage.local`, on your device only, so that rewatching is instant.

You can delete all stored data by removing the extension.
